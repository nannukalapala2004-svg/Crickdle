    How to play Super Over

    Every day there are six multiple-choice questions about the IPL, the same for everyone. Each question is a ball of the over, and you score runs like a batter: the faster you pick the right answer, the more runs.

    Wrong answer, or no answer in 30 seconds0 (dot ball)

    The best possible score is 36: six sixes. The clock starts when you press Face the first ball, and for each later ball when it appears. It keeps running if you leave the page. After each ball you see the right answer and a fact; press Next ball when you're ready, as the next clock only starts then.

    Questions cover IPL champions and finals, Player of the Match awards, the season's top run-scorers and wicket-takers, career records, debuts and the teams players played for. Every answer is worked out from the ball-by-ball records of every IPL match from 2008 to 2026.

    A new Super Over arrives at midnight your local time. Practice gives you a random over any time and doesn't count towards your stats. Your streak counts the days in a row you play.

    Records count IPL matches only, including playoffs; super overs are left out. Delhi Daredevils, Kings XI Punjab and Royal Challengers Bangalore appear under their current names (DC, PBKS, RCB).
