Six cricket trivia questions a day: one over, six balls. How many can you hit?

    How to play The Over

    Every day there are six multiple-choice questions about the IPL, the same for everyone. Each question is a ball of the over. Pick an answer: you get one try per ball, then see the right answer and a fact before the next ball.

    Questions cover IPL champions and finals, Player of the Match awards, the season's top run-scorers and wicket-takers, career records, debuts and the teams players played for. Every answer is worked out from the ball-by-ball records of every IPL match from 2008 to 2026.

    A new over arrives at midnight your local time. Practice gives you a random over any time and doesn't count towards your stats. Your streak counts the days in a row you play.

    Records count IPL matches only, including playoffs; super overs are left out. Top run-scorer and wicket-taker questions count every match of that season. Delhi Daredevils, Kings XI Punjab and Royal Challengers Bangalore appear under their current names (DC, PBKS, RCB).

    Wicketle is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the IPL, the BCCI or any team.
