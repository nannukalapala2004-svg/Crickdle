    Colour guide

    CSK

    Green means correct. This attribute exactly matches the hidden player.

    4842▲

    Yellow means close or partly right. A number is near the answer, a role overlaps (all-rounder against batter), the two players share a franchise, or the best bowling has the same number of wickets.

    AUS

    Red means wrong. Rule this value out and move on.

    • ▲ means the answer's number is higher than your guess (for best bowling, the answer's figure is better). ▼ means lower or worse.
    • * after a top score means not out, like 175*.
    • – means the player never batted (top score) or never bowled (best bowling) in the IPL.
    • A grey-blue ? tile on Age means we don't have a birth date for one of the two players, so Age gives no clue in that row.
    • † after an age means the player has died; the age shown is their age at death.

    How to play Player of the Day

    1. Play the daily puzzle. Full Toss mode has one player of the day, the same for everyone. For more games, tap the Mode button and pick Good Length, Yorker, Spot the Star or 2026 season: these are practice modes with a new random player every game.
    2. Type at least two letters of any IPL player's name, first name or surname, in the search box.
    3. Choose a player from the list by tapping it, or with the arrow keys and Enter.
    4. Read the row. Twelve tiles light up green, yellow or red under the column names at the top of the board, comparing your guess with the hidden player. On a phone, swipe the board sideways to see every column.
    5. Want a simpler board? Press Less stats at the top right of the board to hide Debut, Matches, Top score, Best bowling and Titles, leaving seven tiles that fit a phone without swiping. Press it again to bring them back.
    6. Follow the arrows on number tiles to go higher or lower with your next guess.
    7. Keep guessing until the Player tile turns green. You have 8 guesses.
    8. Share your result with Copy result. It copies a spoiler-free grid of coloured squares.
    9. Come back tomorrow. A new player arrives at midnight your local time. Want more now? Pick Good Length, Yorker, Spot the Star or 2026 season from the Mode menu for unlimited random games.

    Tips & strategy

    Wicketle tips

    • Open with a well-travelled player. Someone who played for many franchises, like Dinesh Karthik or Aaron Finch, makes the Team tile turn yellow far more often, which quickly shows which franchises to focus on.
    • Use an all-rounder early. A yellow Role against an all-rounder tells you the answer is a pure batter or bowler. Then check Runs and Wickets to see which.
    • Settle Country first. A red India tile means an overseas player, which cuts the list a lot. Each team fields only four overseas players, so the pool is much smaller.
    • Using Less stats and stuck? Switch it off. The five extra columns give you more to work with, especially Titles and Best bowling.
    • Let Debut and Age set the era. Debut and Age arrows together tell you whether you're looking for a 2008 original, a mid-2010s regular or a newer face. A young age with an early debut points to someone who started as a teenager.
    • Read Top score and Best bowling together. A dash on your guess's Best bowling with a ▲ means the answer has bowled, so think bowlers and all-rounders.
    • Titles narrow things fast. Many players have 0. Three or more usually points to the CSK and MI dynasties.
    • In 2026 season mode, a green Team means the answer was in that franchise's 2026 side, which leaves only about 20 players.

    General strategy

    • Make every guess work. Pick a player who could be the answer and also tests something new, rather than repeating what you already know.
    • Halve the range. If Runs says ▲ from 1,000 and ▼ from 5,000, aim near 3,000 next.
    • Trust the reds. A red value is ruled out completely, so don't guess another player with it.
    • Use the practice modes to learn the numbers. Random games build a feel for who has how many runs and wickets.
    • Play daily for your streak. Your streak grows with every daily puzzle you solve on its day.

    Rules

    • You get 8 guesses per puzzle.
    • There is one daily puzzle, in Full Toss mode, the same for every player. It resets at midnight your local time. Each day's player is picked at random from all Full Toss players, so a player can come up again on a later day.
    • The other modes are practice modes with a random player every game. In Full Toss, Good Length and Spot the Star you can only guess players from that mode. In Yorker and 2026 season you can guess any IPL player.
    • You can't guess the same player twice in one game.
    • Practice games don't count towards your stats or streak.
    • Missed a day? Open the Archive to play any earlier puzzle. Archive games don't count towards your streak.
    • Stats and streaks are saved on this device only. Clearing your browser data resets them.
    • Stats include every IPL match from 2008 to 2026, including playoffs. Super overs are left out.
